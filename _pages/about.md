The Chemical Crystallography Group (CCG) aims to promote and support the activities of the chemical crystallography community within the UK and Eire. Our group supports annual meetings of the UK crystallography community in the spring, and a dedicated chemical crystallography meeting each autumn. 

In addition, we award an annual Young Crystallographer Prize, sponsored by the Cambridge Structural Database.
