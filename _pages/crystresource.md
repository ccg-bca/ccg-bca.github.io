There are many fantastic resources available for teaching and learning about crystallography, and to help in crystallography-related research activities. You can find some of these resources below!


# Educational Resources
* <a href="https://learn.crystallography.org.uk/">
  <span style="color: blue;">British Crystallography Association Educational Resources</span>
</a>  


# Research Resources
* <a href="https://ccg.crystallography.org.uk/crystallography-resources/#:~:text=Bilbao%20Crystallography%20Server">
  <span style="color: blue;">Bilbao Crystallography Server</span>
</a> -- contains many useful resources for analysing and converting crystallographic structures. 

* <a href="https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/structures/">
  <span style="color: blue;">Cambridge Crystallography Database Crystal Structure Search</span>
</a> -- Online Repository of CCDC crystal structures. 

* <a href="https://www.crystallography.net/cod/">
  <span style="color: blue;">Crystallography Open Database</span>
</a> -- Open-access collection of crystal structures of organic, inorganic, metal-organic compounds and minerals. 

* <a href="https://www.olexsys.org/olex2/docs/">
  <span style="color: blue;">Olex2</span>
</a> -- User-friendly GUI for solution and refinement of single crystal diffraction data. 

* <a href="https://xray.chem.wisc.edu/idealized-molecular-geometry-library/">
  <span style="color: blue;">Idealised Molecular Geometry Library</span>
</a> -- provide facile access to idealized geometries of molecules, ions, and molecular fragments that may cause problems during crystallographic structural refinement.
